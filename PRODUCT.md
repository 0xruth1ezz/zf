# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the crawler operator. They use the interface to check zFrontier automation status, verify lottery engagements and daily sign-ins, and manage the accounts that the crawler uses.

## Product Purpose

This product automates zFrontier lottery participation and daily sign-ins, then exposes a small operational surface for monitoring what happened and maintaining account credentials. Success means the operator can quickly answer what ran, what was recorded, what account was involved, and whether configuration needs attention.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, operational. The interface should feel like a trusted internal tool: calm enough for repeated checks, crisp enough for data review, and direct enough that account changes feel controlled.

## Anti-references

Do not make this feel like a flashy SaaS marketing dashboard. Avoid decorative motion, ornamental cards, hero-style metric blocks, heavy gradients, noisy shadows, and UI flourishes that slow down operational reading.

## Design Principles

- Lead with operational clarity: status, counts, timestamps, and account identity should be easy to scan.
- Keep controls predictable: forms, filters, navigation, and tables should use familiar product UI patterns.
- Use restraint to build trust: visual emphasis should indicate current location, primary action, or state, not decoration.
- Design for repeat use: dense information is acceptable when hierarchy and spacing make it readable.
- Make failure and emptiness useful: missing records, empty account lists, and configuration gaps should explain what the operator can do next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target practical WCAG AA defaults: readable contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-friendly navigation and forms, responsive tables, and reduced-motion support for any transitions.
